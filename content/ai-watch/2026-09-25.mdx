Chip stocks closed a volatile week with small gains. Broadcom rose 0.70% to $352.81, Nvidia 0.22% to $225.07, AMD 0.22% to $630.63 and Micron 0.16% to $1,082.28. Oracle fell 1.75% to $137.10, a third straight decline.

The trade headline was the main support. The U.S. and China agreed to extend their truce to January 10, 2027, and President Trump called his meeting with Xi Jinping "very productive." Details on chips were not part of the public reports.

For the week, AMD finished at $630.63 against $559.82 the prior Friday, a gain of about 12.6%. Nvidia was up about 1.3% and Broadcom was down about 1.3%.

## The number behind it

**$137.10.** The decline since Oracle's September 17 close of $150.59 is about 9%. The stock is carrying the market's concerns about debt-funded AI buildouts more visibly than any other large name.

## What to watch

Next week's jobs data, since a strong report would raise October rate-hike odds, and any detail on chip provisions from the U.S.-China truce.
