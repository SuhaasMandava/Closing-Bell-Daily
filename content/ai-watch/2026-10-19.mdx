A modest recovery. Micron gained 2.97% to $904.90, Broadcom 1.78% to $334.59, AMD 1.76% to $538.05, Nvidia 1.38% to $217.09 and Oracle 1.06% to $129.59. The semiconductor ETF SOXX gained about 2.4% after a weekly drop of 11.8%, its worst since March, according to market coverage.

Press reports described the rebound as positioning ahead of hyperscaler earnings and a planned equity raise at Meta. The sources reviewed did not identify one company-specific catalyst.

## The number behind it

**-11.8%.** That is how far SOXX fell the previous week, which makes Monday's 2.4% bounce a recovery of about a fifth. The group is still well below its early-October level: AMD is 17% below its October 6 close, and Micron 18% below October 1.

## What to watch

The AI trade's earnings test arrives this week. Meta's results, due this week according to press reports, will show whether capex guidance rises again.
