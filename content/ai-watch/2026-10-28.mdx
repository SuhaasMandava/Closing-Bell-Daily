Oracle gave investors the numbers they had asked for. At its investor day, the company set a fiscal 2030 revenue target above $225 billion, said it expects positive free cash flow in the second half of fiscal 2028 and at least $30 billion of free cash flow in fiscal 2029, and capped new capital-market funding at $20 billion through fiscal 2030, according to market coverage. The stock rose 10.73% to $144.54.

Micron rose 5.86% to $983.23, AMD 3.24% to $578.44 and Broadcom 2.77% to $351.81. Nvidia fell 1.17% to $215.38.

## The number behind it

**$20 billion.** Oracle's cap on new funding is a response to a month in which its stock fell from $150.59 to $125.54 on financing worries. Combined with the free cash flow targets, it puts a boundary on the debt story that has weighed on the stock.
