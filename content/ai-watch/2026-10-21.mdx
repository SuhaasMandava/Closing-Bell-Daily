AI hardware rallied on a hard number. Super Micro raised its first-quarter fiscal 2027 revenue guidance to $18.5 to $19.5 billion from $14.5 to $15.5 billion, with gross margin of 12% to 12.5% against 10.4% to 10.8% before. The stock rose 9.6%, Dell 5.3% and Hewlett Packard Enterprise 3.7%.

Nvidia gained 2.54% to $221.97, AMD 3.36% to $552.72, Micron 1.96% to $913.67 and Broadcom 1.03% to $337.80. Oracle fell 1.53% to $129.13.

## The number behind it

**$4 billion.** Super Micro lifted its quarterly revenue midpoint by roughly that amount, about 27%. For Nvidia and AMD, whose accelerators go into those servers, a raise like that suggests demand is holding even as financing questions persist.
