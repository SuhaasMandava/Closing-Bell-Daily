Nvidia's slide continued as its rivals held up. Nvidia fell 5.47% to $168.89, its lowest close in this period, while Broadcom rose 3.91% to $331.98 and AMD 1.30% to $515.21. Micron slipped 1.10% to $725.11 and Oracle 1.51% to $117.98.

The sources reviewed did not give a new Nvidia-specific reason beyond the board investigation announced the day before. The 10-year yield jumped to 4.98% on hawkish comments from the Dallas Fed's Lorie Logan.

## The number behind it

**$168.89.** Nvidia's close is 29% below its October 6 close of $239.24, and 20% below its September 14 close of $210.96. AMD, in contrast, is 21% below its own October 6 level.
