A quiet day for the group. Oracle rose 1.20% to $131.14, while Nvidia slipped 0.29% to $216.47, AMD 0.62% to $534.74, Broadcom 0.07% to $334.37 and Micron 0.98% to $896.07.

Press reports said investors were focused on hyperscaler earnings and Meta's plan to raise equity to pay for AI infrastructure. The sources reviewed did not identify a company-specific catalyst.

## The number behind it

**$896.07.** Micron's close is 18% below its October 1 high of $1,097.39, and AMD is 18% below its October 6 close. The chip group has not recovered what it lost last week, and the coming earnings from its biggest customers are the test.

## What to watch

The first big hyperscaler results this week. Spending guidance matters more than revenue for this group.
