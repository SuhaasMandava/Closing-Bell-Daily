Anthropic's debut was strong and did not lift the chip stocks. The company priced its IPO at $83 a share, above the $70 to $78 range it had marketed, for a valuation of about $1.5 trillion, according to press reports. It began trading near noon as ANT. Zacks reported that shares rose as much as 34% at first and ended up 17% from the first trade of $111.50.

The chip names gave back any early lift. AMD fell 7.72% to $489.19, Nvidia 3.30% to $145.98, Oracle 2.92% to $110.57, Micron 2.10% to $690.44 and Broadcom 2.09% to $346.65.

## The number behind it

**$1.5 trillion.** Anthropic's IPO valuation is very large, and it comes as the chip stocks that supply AI have fallen sharply. AMD is 25% below its October 6 close, Micron 37% below October 1, and Nvidia 39% below October 6.

For the four sessions since November 13, Nvidia fell 10%, AMD 8%, Micron 11% and Oracle 12%, while Broadcom was flat.

## What to watch

How ANT trades in its first full week, and whether the market treats the offering as a sign of strength or the top of the cycle. Next week brings the Thanksgiving holiday and a shortened session.
