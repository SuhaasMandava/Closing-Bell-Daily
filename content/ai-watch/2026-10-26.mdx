Two pieces of AI financing news lifted the chip stocks. Anthropic filed an S-1 for a planned Nasdaq listing under the ticker ANT. It disclosed revenue of $16.29 billion for the first half of 2026, compared with $1.16 billion a year earlier. Meta said it would raise $95 billion for AI infrastructure.

Nvidia rose 2.39% to $216.00, AMD 2.80% to $531.28, Broadcom 2.33% to $329.17 and Micron 2.98% to $903.70. Oracle slipped 0.08% to $126.06.

SpaceX also said it had completed a $40 billion debt financing for chips used for AI, and it is a large Nvidia customer.

## The number behind it

**$16.29 billion.** Anthropic's first-half revenue, according to its filing as summarized by Zacks, answers part of the demand question raised by the OpenAI revenue report on October 8. It is a number from a private company's offering document, and a listing would add public-market scrutiny of its spending commitments.

## What to watch

Alphabet's and Amazon's results later this week, the Fed on Wednesday and the details of the Meta offering.
