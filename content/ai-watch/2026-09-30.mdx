September ended with wide dispersion inside the AI group. AMD closed at $611.76, up about 30% for the month. Micron rose about 11% to $1,065.11 and Nvidia about 3.4% to $228.38. Broadcom lost about 5.2% to $351.19 and Oracle about 7.9% to $137.30.

On the last day of the month, Nvidia rose 0.51% and AMD 0.69%, while Broadcom fell 1.10% and Oracle 0.36%. Micron was unchanged. Synopsys gained 4.8%, the Nasdaq's best performer.

For the third quarter, Nvidia rose about 14%, AMD 5%, Broadcom fell 7% and Micron 8%.

## The number behind it

**14.1%.** That is Nvidia's gain for the quarter, from $200.09 on June 30. It shows the split inside the AI trade: the company with the largest accelerator share outperformed, while the infrastructure and financing-linked names fell as long-term yields rose above 5%.

## What to watch

Micron's earnings, due this week, with the memory group trading at a high multiple to record profits. October starts with Friday's jobs report.
