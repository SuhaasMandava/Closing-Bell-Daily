The chip group gave back part of its week's gains after Alphabet's results. The parent of Google posted a free cash flow of negative $9.1 billion for the quarter, against an expected negative $3.5 billion, missed on earnings and revenue, and fell 12.6%.

Micron fell 4.98% to $934.27, Oracle 3.45% to $139.56, AMD 2.08% to $566.42, Broadcom 1.94% to $345.00 and Nvidia 0.80% to $213.66.

ServiceNow fell 27% after guiding for slower remaining-performance-obligation growth of 18.5% against 19.4% expected, which added to worries about software's AI returns.

## The number behind it

**-$9.1 billion.** Alphabet's free cash flow shows the same pattern as Meta's a week earlier: spending on AI exceeds the cash the business generates. The chip names benefit from the spending but have fallen each time a large customer's balance sheet became the subject.

## What to watch

Amazon's results after the close on Thursday and October's jobs report next week.
