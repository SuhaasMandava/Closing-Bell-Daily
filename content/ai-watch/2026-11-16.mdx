Chip stocks extended their slide. Nvidia fell 4.12% to $156.14 on reports that its board does not plan to reinstate former CEO Jensen Huang. Micron fell 7.62% to $719.02, AMD 5.58% to $504.28, Oracle 4.99% to $119.09 and Broadcom 3.92% to $333.40. Intel fell 7%.

Zacks said the Trump administration implemented a new rule restricting China's access to AI chips from major U.S. tech companies, and that it could be a major gamechanger for chipmakers' fortunes. The sources reviewed did not give the rule's details.

## The number behind it

**43%.** The Philadelphia Semiconductor index was up 106.6% for the year in mid-June and is now up about 43%, after a 5.3% fall on Monday. Nvidia, at $156.14, is 35% below its October 6 close.

## What to watch

Nvidia's results on Tuesday after the close, which will be the first since Huang's departure. Anthropic's IPO this week will also affect demand for AI exposure.
