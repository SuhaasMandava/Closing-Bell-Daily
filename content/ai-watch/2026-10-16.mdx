The chip rout deepened. The Philadelphia Semiconductor index fell about 3.8%, its eighth straight losing session and longest streak since 2008, with a decline of about 16% over the eight days, according to press reports. AMD fell 5.95% to $528.75, Micron 5.63% to $878.84, Oracle 4.64% to $128.23, Broadcom 4.52% to $328.75 and Nvidia 1.43% to $214.13.

Reports pointed to AI spending doubts, funding worries, higher oil and yields, and Chinese competition in memory. The Nasdaq lost 2.15% and the VIX rose 21%.

## The number behind it

**8.** The number of straight losses in the semiconductor index, the longest since 2008. The streak had no single trigger. It fit a week with hot CPI, a revenue report on OpenAI, rising oil and worries about funding and Chinese competition.

## What to watch

Whether earnings from the large chip and cloud companies later in the month restore confidence. Oracle's financing is the particular pressure point.
