Chip stocks split by company. Nvidia rose 1.68% to $228.86, a rare gain in a down session. AMD fell 3.61% to $607.87, Micron 2.61% to $1,053.98, Broadcom 0.92% to $349.57 and Oracle 3.28% to $132.60. Arm Holdings was the biggest loser on the Nasdaq, down 8.7%.

The sources reviewed did not give a company-specific reason for Arm's drop. It came on a day when the 10-year yield rose to 5.24%, and Arm trades at one of the higher multiples in the group.

Oracle's fall extends a decline from $150.59 on September 17 to $132.60, a loss of about 12% in seven sessions.

## The number behind it

**$132.60.** That is Oracle's close, the lowest in the stretch of sessions covered here. The stock has become the market's proxy for AI debt concerns as yields rise.

## What to watch

Micron's fiscal fourth-quarter results are due this week, and the memory group has moved the chip tape in both directions this month. Friday's payrolls matter for the rate path.
