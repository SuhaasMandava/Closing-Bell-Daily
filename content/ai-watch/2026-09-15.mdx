The pressure on the AI trade shifted from sentiment to financing. With the 30-year yield at 5.40% and the 10-year at 5.04% intraday, both the highest since 2007, investors worried about heavily indebted companies, particularly those funding AI infrastructure with borrowed money.

The effect was uneven. AMD rose 2.19% to $504.20 and Nvidia gained 0.57% to $212.17, while Broadcom fell 1.58% to $339.27 and Oracle lost 3.07% to $140.35. Microsoft slid 1.64%.

Oracle's move is the clearest example of the day's theme. The company has been among the most aggressive spenders on AI capacity, so a rising-rate backdrop puts its funding costs in question.

## The number behind it

**$1.5 trillion.** AI-related companies have accounted for more than that in new debt issuance this year, a figure cited in market coverage in recent sessions. At that scale, every move in long-term yields changes the cost of the buildout, and the 30-year at 5.40% is a number the sector has not had to carry before.

## What to watch

Wednesday's Fed statement. A signal of further hikes would raise borrowing costs again for exactly the companies that have been financing AI expansion through bond markets.
