A modest rebound day for the AI names that had fallen hardest. Oracle rose 3.91% to $137.79, Broadcom gained 1.58% to $355.10 and Micron 1.05% to $1,065.08. Nvidia slipped 0.72% to $227.21 and AMD 0.05% to $607.57.

The Nasdaq fell 0.09%, so the group's moves were company-specific rather than part of a broad tech rally. The sources reviewed did not identify a catalyst for Oracle's bounce.

## The number behind it

**5.26%.** The 10-year yield's close is the highest since 2007, and it makes the relative strength in Oracle notable. The stock had been the most rate-sensitive name in the group, and a bounce on a day of higher yields suggests selling was getting stretched.

## What to watch

Micron's earnings and Wednesday's PCE data. Oracle and Broadcom are the two large names most exposed to the debt-financing debate, and both have moved sharply this month.
