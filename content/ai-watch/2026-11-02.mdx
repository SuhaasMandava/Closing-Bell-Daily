The AI selloff extended into November. Oracle fell 8.76% to $119.22, giving back its entire investor-day gain from October 28. AMD lost 7.52% to $466.84, Micron 5.78% to $833.59, Nvidia 1.26% to $202.06 and Broadcom 1.18% to $322.23.

Zacks cited concerns over costly AI investments, stretched valuations and disappointing earnings outlooks. The Nasdaq-100 closed more than 10% below its October 6 record high.

## The number behind it

**-28%.** AMD's close of $466.84 compares with $649.42 on October 6, a drop of about 28% in four weeks. Oracle is down about 18% from its October 28 close of $144.54 in three sessions.

## What to watch

Tuesday's midterm elections, with Democrats favored in the House and the Senate competitive. Zacks noted the possibility of closer scrutiny of AI companies and data centers if control changes.
