Falling rate-hike odds lifted the AI names that had been hurt most by yields. Broadcom rose 3.35% to $355.14, Oracle 3.06% to $142.30 and AMD 2.95% to $633.91. Nvidia gained 1.34% to $233.95. Micron fell 2.05% to $1,074.89 after Thursday's gain.

## What moved

| Name | Change | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AVGO | +3.35% | Best of the group |
| ORCL | +3.06% | Extended its bounce |
| AMD | +2.95% | Back above $630 |
| NVDA | +1.34% | Record close for the sample |
| MU | -2.05% | Gave back part of Thursday |

The pattern was consistent with the month's theme: when yields rise, Oracle and Broadcom lead the decline, and when rate-hike odds fall, they lead the bounce. The sources reviewed did not identify any company news for the moves.

## The number behind it

**29,000.** September payroll growth, against a forecast of 79,000, left CME FedWatch pricing a 77.9% chance that the Fed holds in October. For heavily financed AI names, that matters more than any individual data point.

## What to watch

The September CPI report is the next big macro event. On the company side, the next earnings catalysts are the large cloud providers' reports later in October.
