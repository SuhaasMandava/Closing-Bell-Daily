The AI trade sold off hard on a day when the buyers' spending, not demand, was the concern. Meta reported negative free cash flow of $2.12 billion, raised the low end of 2026 capex guidance to $135 billion and signaled equity issuance to fund AI. AMD fell 8.10% to $507.93, Micron 6.26% to $856.49, Nvidia 4.40% to $212.21, Broadcom 4.31% to $323.24 and Oracle 2.78% to $125.54.

Press reports said Jensen Huang's comments on capital shortages and a supply warning from Lam Research added to the pressure.

## The number behind it

**$34.91 billion.** Meta spent that on AI capital expenditures in one quarter, and it raised the low end of its annual range to $135 billion. The chip complex benefits from the spending, but the shares fell as investors focused on whether it can be financed without damaging the balance sheet.

## What to watch

The 10-year yield dropped 19 basis points to 4.95%, a sharp fall that did not help. Next are the Fed meeting and the other hyperscalers' reports.
