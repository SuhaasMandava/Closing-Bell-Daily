Chips led the broad market higher. AMD jumped 6.36% to $545.09 and Intel 7.67% to $108.80, with Micron up 5.50% to $977.50. Nvidia gained 2.54% to $219.34, Broadcom rose 2.29% to $347.30, and TSMC added 3.00%. Oracle rallied 5.19% and Microsoft 1.52%.

AI server names rose too. Press reports put Hewlett Packard Enterprise up about 9%, Super Micro up 6% and Dell up 3%.

The move followed a week in which these stocks had fallen on AI-pacing concerns and rising yields. With the 10-year falling nearly 6 basis points to 4.95%, the pressure on valuations eased on the same day.

## The number behind it

**4.95%.** The fall in the 10-year yield, together with a drop in jobless claims to 196,000, gave the chip complex relief on the discount rate and evidence that demand was holding. Market commentary also pointed to reports of tight chip and memory supply as a supporting factor, though those reports were not confirmed by company statements in the sources reviewed.

## What to watch

Whether Friday's session holds the gains. Micron and the memory group are the ones to watch given the supply talk, and the Fed's tightening path remains the main risk to the multiple.
