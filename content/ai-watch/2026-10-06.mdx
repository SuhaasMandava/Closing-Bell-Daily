The AI bid moved to networking. Ciena, a maker of optical networking gear used in data centers, rose 13.9%, the best performance in the S&P 500. Broadcom gained 3.67% to $375.81, its highest close in this stretch, and AMD 2.80% to $649.42. Oracle rose 1.61% to $144.77 and Nvidia 0.14% to $239.24. Micron fell 1.73%.

The sources reviewed did not give a company-specific reason for Ciena's jump.

## The number behind it

**$375.81.** Broadcom's close is 9% above its September 14 low of $344.72 and up from $343.64 on October 1, a 9.4% gain in three sessions. Broadcom builds the networking chips and custom accelerators used in AI clusters, so strength in Ciena and Broadcom in the same session points to demand for data center connectivity.

## What to watch

The Fed minutes on Wednesday and then the start of earnings season. Whether networking can keep leading depends on the rate path and on updates from the hyperscalers.
