AI stocks missed the rebound. Nvidia fell 1.84% to $217.23, AMD 1.50% to $562.23 and Broadcom 0.55% to $344.33. Oracle gained 3.00% to $134.47 and Micron 1.87% to $931.24.

The S&P 500 and the Dow rose, and the 10-year yield dropped to about 5.09%. The AI names did not follow, and Zacks described the day as a rotation away from debt-sensitive AI stocks.

## The number behind it

**$217.23.** Nvidia's close is 9% below its October 6 peak of $239.24 and above the September 14 low of $210.96. The stock has held better than AMD and Micron, which are 13% and 15% below their highs.
