The Fed minutes left most AI-linked stocks little changed. Micron was the exception, rising 4.06% to $1,088.00. Broadcom gained 0.19% to $376.51, Nvidia fell 0.74% to $237.47, AMD 0.55% to $645.86 and Oracle 0.84% to $143.56.

The sources reviewed did not give a company-specific reason for Micron's gain. It followed two down days and a strong report on October 1.

## The number behind it

**$1,088.00.** Micron's close is about 18% above its September 14 close of $924.03 and 0.9% below its October 1 close of $1,097.39. The stock has moved between roughly $924 and $1,097 in about three weeks.

## What to watch

Thursday's jobless claims and next week's CPI report. For the AI names, the next large catalyst is the late-October earnings from the biggest cloud and chip companies.
