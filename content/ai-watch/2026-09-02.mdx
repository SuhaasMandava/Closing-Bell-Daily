The AI trade rebounded on Wednesday led by gains in market heavyweight Nvidia, as commentary around sovereign AI infrastructure and rising capex estimates offered fresh momentum to chipmakers. Speaking at a public event today, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang emphasized that nations must view artificial intelligence capacity as fundamental infrastructure akin to energy or water systems, framing government spending as a growing revenue pillar. The sector showed resilience despite lingering broader debate over hyper-concentrated infrastructure budgets.

Market participants continue to weigh the sheer velocity of AI infrastructure commitments against near-term monetization timelines. While concentration risks and financing models remain a topic of Wall Street discussion, expanding international buildouts provide semiconductor leaders with broader end-market diversification beyond North American cloud providers.

## The number behind it

Aggregate 2026 capital expenditure across top US hyperscalers is now projected near **$625 billion**, up by over $170 billion since the start of the year. This massive capex figure underscores how major technology firms are directing an estimated 93% of operational cash flow toward AI data centers and GPU clusters.

## What to watch

Investors are focused on Broadcom’s upcoming earnings disclosure to gauge demand for custom AI accelerators and enterprise networking silicon, alongside potential forward guidance updates from tier-1 cloud customers.
