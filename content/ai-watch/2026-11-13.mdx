The rotation into Nvidia's rivals unwound. AMD fell 8.75% to $534.06 and Broadcom 4.76% to $347.01, a day after both stocks jumped on Nvidia's CEO resignation. Micron fell 2.52% to $778.31, Nvidia 2.01% to $162.85 and Oracle 1.38% to $125.35. The Philadelphia Semiconductor index lost 3.4%.

Zacks said Nvidia's slide weighed on the sector and raised new concerns about the group, ahead of Anthropic's IPO expected next week.
