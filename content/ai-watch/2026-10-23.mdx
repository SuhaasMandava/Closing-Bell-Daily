AI stocks held steady after Thursday's drop. AMD rose 1.74% to $516.79, Micron 2.46% to $877.55 and Oracle 0.49% to $126.16. Nvidia fell 0.59% to $210.96 and Broadcom 0.48% to $321.68.

Friday's selling hit cyclicals and defensives rather than technology. The Nasdaq lost only 0.21% while the Dow fell 1.23%.

## The number behind it

**$210.96.** Nvidia closed at exactly the level it hit on September 14, the low of the AI-pacing selloff. The stock is now 12% below its October 6 peak of $239.24.

## What to watch

Hyperscaler earnings continue next week, and the Fed meets Wednesday. Oil is the wild card for yields.
