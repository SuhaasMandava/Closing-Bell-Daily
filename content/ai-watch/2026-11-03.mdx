Chips bounced. The Philadelphia Semiconductor index rose 3%, with Oracle up 5.85% to $126.19, AMD 3.65% to $483.90, Broadcom 3.07% to $332.13, Micron 2.58% to $855.10 and Nvidia 2.29% to $206.69.

Zacks described investors returning to AI shares after a recent selloff and the Nasdaq-100's entry into a correction the day before.

## The number behind it

**$126.19.** Oracle's close sits 13% below its October 28 close after Monday's drop, and it shows how much of the investor-day boost has been lost. The company said on October 28 that it would cap new funding at $20 billion through fiscal 2030.

## What to watch

The midterm results. Investors cited the possibility of closer scrutiny of AI companies and data-center buildouts if Democrats win. That would be a new variable for hardware demand.
