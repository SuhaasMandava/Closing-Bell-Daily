The chip rally paused. Broadcom lost 2.62% to $354.99, Nvidia 1.47% to $225.51, AMD 1.47% to $614.61 and Micron 2.22% to $1,071.88. Oracle fell 3.11% to $144.56.

The cause was macro, not company news. The 10-year yield rose to 5.135% at the high, its highest since 2007, and the Nasdaq lost 1.13%.

## What moved

| Name | Change | Note |

| ORCL | -3.11% | Largest loss of the group |
| AVGO | -2.62% | Gave back Monday's and Tuesday's gains |
| MU | -2.22% | Memory eased from highs |
| NVDA | -1.47% | In line with the Nasdaq decline |
| AMD | -1.47% | Held most of Monday's surge |

AMD's close of $614.61 is still about 25% above its September 14 close of $493.41.

## The number behind it

**5.11%.** The 10-year yield's close is the number for AI-linked stocks right now. Oracle, the most heavily indebted name in the group, fell the most, which fits the pattern seen on other days this month when yields jumped.

## What to watch

Thursday's U.S.-China summit and its possible discussions on AI regulation. Any easing of chip export rules would be positive for the group, and any tightening negative.
