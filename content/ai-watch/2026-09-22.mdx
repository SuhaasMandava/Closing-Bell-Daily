Chip momentum continued on a day the S&P 500 stood still. Micron rose 5.00% to $1,096.16, AMD gained 1.34% to $623.77, Nvidia 0.66% to $228.87 and Broadcom 0.52% to $364.54. The Nasdaq closed at a record for the second straight day.

Market coverage tied the buying to confidence in AI demand after the solid reception of Meta's Muse assistant, which launched earlier in the month. Sandisk, a memory-adjacent name, rose 6.8%.

Memory remains the group's strongest corner. Micron has now risen from $924.03 on September 14 to $1,096.16, a gain of about 19% in six sessions.

## The number behind it

**$1,096.16.** That is Micron's close, up from a low of $924.03 a week earlier. The stock's move shows how fast the AI-pacing scare of September 14 reversed. Investors who sold on the warnings from AI company leaders have since seen demand signals, from Oracle's capex plan to Meta's product launch, pointing the other way.

## What to watch

The U.S.-China summit on Thursday and any change in the 10-year yield, which closed at 4.97%. A move back above 5% has repeatedly cut into the chip rally.
