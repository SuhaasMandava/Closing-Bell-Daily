Chips led a record Nasdaq close. AMD rose 9.95% to $615.52, taking its market value to $1 trillion. Intel gained about 12%, and Nvidia added 2.30% to $227.38. Broadcom rose 1.41% to $362.66 and Micron 2.77% to $1,043.96.

The group had been recovering since the Fed-week selloff. A solid response to Meta's new AI assistant, Muse, launched earlier in the month, has also been cited as a driver of chip demand expectations.

Lower yields were the backdrop: the 10-year fell 3.5 basis points to 4.96% and oil fell to an 11-day low.

## The number behind it

**$1 trillion.** AMD's market value crossing that line comes after a year of steep gains. At $615.52 a share, the stock is up roughly 25% from  its September 14 close of $493.41, a one-week change that is large even for a volatile sector.

## What to watch

Whether the group holds a gain this size when yields move. A U.S.-China summit on Thursday could bring headlines on chip trade rules and AI regulation.
