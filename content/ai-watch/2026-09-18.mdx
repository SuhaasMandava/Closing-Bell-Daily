The chip rebound held for a second day. Micron rose 3.92% to $1,015.80, putting the memory maker above $1,000 a share. Broadcom gained 2.97% to $357.61, AMD 2.70% to $559.82 and Nvidia 1.34% to $222.27. TSMC added 1.02%.

Microsoft fell 0.80% and Oracle lost 1.98%, so the strength was concentrated in the chip suppliers, not the cloud buyers.

Two days of gains recovered most of Monday's AI-pacing selloff for Nvidia, AMD and Broadcom. Nvidia's close of $222.27 compares with $218.29 the previous Friday.

## The number behind it

**25.3 billion.** That was the share volume across U.S. exchanges on Friday, well above the 20-day average of 16.2 billion. Quarterly options expirations typically boost volume and add noise to individual-stock moves, so Friday's gains are best read as part of a two-day rebound rather than a new trend.

## What to watch

Next week's tech calendar is light, so the Treasury market will drive the group. The 10-year closed near 5.00% on Friday, and a move well above it would test the rebound.
