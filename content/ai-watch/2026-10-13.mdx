A split session. Oracle rose 4.23% to $143.20, AMD 1.90% to $605.53, Nvidia 0.97% to $228.52 and Broadcom 0.73% to $357.31. Micron fell 3.62% to $953.19, its lowest close since September 16.

Zacks listed semiconductor selloffs, AI competition concerns and caution ahead of Wednesday's CPI report among the reasons for the market's weakness. The sources reviewed did not give a Micron-specific cause.
