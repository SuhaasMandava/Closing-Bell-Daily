The chip group ended the month with its worst day since the mid-October rout. Intel fell 28.6% after guiding to a weak fourth quarter on slower PC demand. AMD lost 10.88% to $504.80, Broadcom 5.48% to $326.08, Micron 5.30% to $884.74, Nvidia 4.22% to $204.64 and Oracle 6.37% to $130.67.

Zacks said investors were selling AI-related stocks after Alphabet's disappointing results the day before and Amazon's miss.

## The number behind it

**-28.6%.** That is a very large one-day move for a company of Intel's size. Intel makes PC chips and has been trying to build a foundry business, and its forecast pointed to weakness in a part of the market that is not driven by AI.

For October, the Nasdaq fell 5.3%. AMD ended the month at $504.80 against $633.91 on October 2.

## What to watch

Apple's results and the election on November 3. If semiconductors keep falling, the 10% decline that defines a correction in the Nasdaq-100 is near.
