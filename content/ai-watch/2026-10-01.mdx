Micron's results gave the AI trade hard numbers on demand. Revenue was $54.23 billion, up 379.3% from a year earlier and 6.3% above forecasts. Non-GAAP earnings were $33.42 a share, more than 11 times the $3.03 reported a year ago. Micron rose 3.03% to $1,097.39.

Elsewhere in the group, Nvidia gained 1.09% to $230.86 and AMD 0.65% to $615.73. Broadcom fell 2.15% to $343.64 and Oracle rose 0.56%.

The report put hard numbers on the memory shortage: tight DRAM and NAND conditions lifted prices, and AI demand pushed data center SSD revenue to nearly $10 billion, more than two-thirds of NAND revenue.

## The number behind it

**379%.** Micron's year-over-year revenue growth is the figure that explains the market's willingness to pay a high price for memory stocks. It reflects pricing as much as volume. The stock was already above $1,000 a share, so the reaction of 3% shows how much of the results were priced in.

## What to watch

Whether Micron's guidance drives estimates higher for SK Hynix, Samsung and Sandisk. Friday's payrolls are the next macro test.
