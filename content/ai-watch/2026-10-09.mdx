A partial recovery. Oracle bounced 4.21% to $141.40, recovering about 70% of Thursday's decline. Broadcom edged up 0.39% to $361.54. Nvidia fell 0.52% to $229.28, AMD 2.03% to $608.10 and Micron 0.66% to $1,029.00.

The Roundhill Magnificent Seven ETF gained 1% and "AI-related momentum stocks" rallied overall, according to market coverage, while the chip suppliers lagged the cloud names.

## The number behind it

**$141.40.** Oracle's close sits between Thursday's $135.69 and its $144.77 peak of October 6, showing how much of the OpenAI selloff was reversed. The suppliers did not recover, which suggests investors still want proof that spending is rising as fast as expected.

## What to watch

Third-quarter earnings season starts next week, and the large cloud providers report in late October. Capex guidance there will address the revenue doubts directly.
