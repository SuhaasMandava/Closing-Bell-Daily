Chip stocks had one of their worst days on record. The Philadelphia Semiconductor index closed down about 12%, and press reports put the market value erased at roughly $1.5 trillion. AMD fell 17.26% to $404.40, Micron 17.46% to $656.39, Broadcom 10.43% to $291.53, Nvidia 9.26% to $181.60 and Oracle 6.93% to $115.16.

Coverage tied the selloff to the aftermath of the midterms. Democratic wins in states where opposition to new data centers has gained ground raised fears of policy changes that could slow the buildout. No single regulatory proposal was cited in the sources reviewed.

## The number behind it

**38%.** AMD's close of $404.40 is that far below its October 6 close of $649.42. Micron is down 40% from its October 1 close of $1,097.39. Nvidia is 24% below its October 6 close of $239.24.

## What to watch

Whether the market treats Thursday as a one-day liquidation or a new regime. The next catalyst is Friday's jobs report, and then any concrete proposals on data-center oversight.
