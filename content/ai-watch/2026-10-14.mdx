The CPI report landed hardest on the AI companies that rely on borrowing. Oracle fell 8.83% to $130.55, its lowest close in this stretch. AMD dropped 5.73% to $570.82, Micron 4.10% to $914.13, Nvidia 3.16% to $221.30 and Broadcom 3.10% to $346.25.

Press reports said CoreWeave, Nebius and Dell also sold off as hotter inflation raised expectations of another Fed increase and higher borrowing costs for AI infrastructure firms.

## The number behind it

**3.7%.** September CPI inflation at that level, above the 3.6% forecast, pushed rate expectations up, and debt-financed AI projects feel the effect most directly. Oracle has fallen 13% from its $150.59 close on September 17 and about 10% from the $144.77 reached on October 6.

## What to watch

Whether the 10-year yield, at 5.19%, drifts back down, and whether the funding debate extends to credit markets.
