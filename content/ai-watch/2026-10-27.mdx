The chip rebound broadened. The Philadelphia Semiconductor index gained 3.6% and the iShares Semiconductor ETF 3.9%. AMD rose 5.45% to $560.26, Broadcom 4.00% to $342.33, Oracle 3.55% to $130.53, Micron 2.77% to $928.76 and Nvidia 0.90% to $217.94.

Zacks tied the move to the AI trade's rebound on Anthropic's IPO filing, and it cited an unusually strong consumer report and lower oil as supporting factors.
