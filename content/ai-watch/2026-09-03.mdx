Broadcom's fiscal third-quarter report, released after Tuesday's close, showed AI semiconductor revenue up 221% year over year to roughly $16.7 billion, but the stock fell 2.74% on Thursday as investors focused on softer margin commentary and a same-day disclosure that Google had struck a $12.2 billion equity-linked custom-chip deal with Marvell. Nvidia, meanwhile, confirmed a definitive agreement to acquire open-source AI hub Hugging Face for roughly $12.9 billion.

Broadcom's headline AI growth rate was hard to argue with, but the stock's reaction underlines how quickly the market has moved to price competitive risk in custom AI silicon — the same Google relationship that has powered Broadcom's AI segment is now also flowing, in part, toward Marvell.

## The number behind it

Broadcom's AI semiconductor revenue reached approximately $16.7 billion for the quarter, up 221% year over year — nearly three times the size of AMD's comparable AI business by growth rate, according to sell-side commentary following the print. Separately, Google's new custom-chip arrangement with Marvell was valued at $12.2 billion in equity-linked terms, prompting several analysts to trim Broadcom's price target on execution and competitive-share concerns even as the underlying AI demand backdrop stayed strong.

## What to watch

Broadcom is scheduled to present at the Goldman Sachs Communicopia and Technology Conference on September 8, an opportunity for CEO Hock Tan to address the margin path and the shape of its multiyear hyperscaler backlog before the next hard data point: Broadcom's fiscal fourth-quarter report on December 9. On the Nvidia side, the Hugging Face deal is expected to close in the first half of 2027, subject to regulatory approval.
