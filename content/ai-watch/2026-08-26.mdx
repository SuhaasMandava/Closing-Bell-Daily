Analytical commentary highlighted surging compute requirements from cloud hyperscalers and sovereign nation infrastructure programs as key structural tailwinds. The beat alleviated short-term concerns around customer capex digestion or slowing deployment velocity.

## The number behind it

Nvidia reported adjusted EPS of $2.22 versus consensus estimates of $2.09, while quarterly revenue grew 105.9% year-over-year to hit $96.22 billion (surpassing $92.27 billion estimates). Datacenter segment revenue drove the vast majority of top-line expansion.

## What to watch
