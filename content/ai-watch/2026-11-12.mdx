Nvidia announced that Jensen Huang was stepping down as president and CEO and leaving its board, effective immediately. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO while the board searches for a permanent chief, and Harvey C. Jones is the first independent chairman. The company said operations, customer commitments and product roadmaps would continue uninterrupted.

The stock fell 4.34% to $166.19. AMD rose 5.86% to $585.30 and Broadcom 5.54% to $364.36, while Micron fell 1.59% to $798.44 and Oracle 0.99% to $127.11.
