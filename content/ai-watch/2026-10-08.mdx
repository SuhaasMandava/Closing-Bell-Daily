The AI trade faced a demand scare. OpenAI told investors its annualized revenue was roughly $50 billion at the end of September, below the $68 to $70 billion widely reported earlier, according to market coverage. A person familiar with the matter said the higher figure had included gross revenue from partners.

Oracle fell 5.48% to $135.69, Micron 4.79% to $1,035.84, Broadcom 4.35% to $360.14, AMD 3.90% to $620.68 and Nvidia 2.94% to $230.48.

The report also included growth figures: OpenAI's run rate grew 77% in its third quarter, and its enterprise business 107%.

## The number behind it

**$50 billion vs $68 billion.** The gap is about $18 billion of annualized revenue, and it matters because Oracle and chip suppliers have priced in demand tied to OpenAI's spending. The explanation that the larger figure counted partner revenue limits the damage but does not remove the question about how fast demand is growing.

The Nasdaq fell 1.25%. Rising oil and yields also played a part, according to some coverage.

## What to watch

Any follow-up from OpenAI or its partners on the revenue figures, and next week's CPI report.
