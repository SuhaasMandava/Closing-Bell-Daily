Chip stocks fell for a second day. Micron lost 3.88% to $989.04, closing below $1,000 for the first time since September 17. AMD fell 2.28% to $594.24, Broadcom 1.88% to $354.73, Oracle 2.84% to $137.39 and Nvidia 1.29% to $226.32. The technology sector fell 0.9%.

The sources reviewed did not identify company-specific news. Brent crude rose to $106.65 and a Fed official called for faster tightening, a combination that has hit the most richly valued names first.

## The number behind it

**-8.5%.** AMD's close of $594.24 is that far below its October 6 close of $649.42. Micron is down 9.9% from its October 1 close of $1,097.39. Both have slid since the OpenAI revenue report on October 8.
