Nvidia was the only large chip name to fall. Its board launched an independent investigation after a New York Times report about CEO Jensen Huang, and the stock lost 4.27% to $178.67. The company said it had no reason to believe national security or company secrets had been compromised.

The rest of the group surged. AMD rose 13.26% to $508.58, Broadcom 8.78% to $319.48, Micron 6.25% to $733.15 and Oracle 3.18% to $119.79. Market coverage called it Broadcom's best day since its deal with Alphabet last November and said investors were rotating toward Nvidia's rivals.

## The number behind it

**13.26%.** AMD's gain pushed the stock to $508.58 from $449.04, but it remains 22% below its October 6 close. Nvidia is 25% below its October 6 close at $178.67.

## What to watch

The investigation's progress and the October CPI report on Tuesday. The group is trading on headlines, and the swings may continue.
