Nvidia and Broadcom led the Nasdaq to a record. Nvidia rose 2.12% to $238.90, its highest close in this run, and Broadcom 2.08% to $362.51. Oracle edged up 0.13% to $142.48, while AMD slipped 0.34% to $631.75 and Micron 1.02% to $1,063.96.

Microsoft rose 1.5%. The sources reviewed pointed to no specific AI news for the day. The gains came on a day of falling volatility and a modest rise in the 10-year yield to 5.31%.

## The number behind it

**$238.90.** Nvidia's close is 13% above its September 14 close of $210.96, and 19% above its $200.09 close on June 30. The stock's rise through the last month tracks the Nasdaq's return to records.
