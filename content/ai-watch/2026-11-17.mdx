A holding pattern before the main event. Broadcom rose 2.46% to $341.60. Nvidia slipped 0.44% to $155.46, AMD 0.17% to $503.41, Micron 0.92% to $712.42 and Oracle 3.43% to $115.00.

Nvidia was due to report fiscal third-quarter results after the close, less than a week after CEO Jensen Huang resigned. Zacks described the day as a continuation of a tech selloff, with semiconductors suffering most over the past week.

## The number behind it

**$115.00.** Oracle's close is 20% below its October 28 close of $144.54 and 24% below its September 17 close of $150.59. The stock has given up the entire gain from its investor day.
