Nvidia's first report since Jensen Huang's resignation was strong, and the stock did not care. Earnings were $2.68 a share against the $2.47 consensus, and revenue of $115.84 billion for the quarter ended October beat estimates by 7.07%, according to Zacks. The company also raised its revenue forecast. The stock fell 2.89% to $150.96.

AMD gained 5.31% to $530.14 and Broadcom 3.64% to $354.05, while Micron slipped 1.00% to $705.27 and Oracle 0.97% to $113.89.

## The number behind it

**$150.96.** Nvidia's close is 37% below its October 6 close, even after a revenue beat and a raised forecast. The stock fell a week after the company lost its CEO. Investors have focused on financing and policy risk more than on reported demand.

## What to watch

Anthropic's IPO, which is expected to price on Wednesday night, a listing that investors will treat as a test for AI valuations.
