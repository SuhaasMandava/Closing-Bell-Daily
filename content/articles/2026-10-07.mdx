The minutes did not change the story, but they confirmed it. The S&P 500 fell 0.22% to 7,801.77, the Nasdaq slipped 0.22% to 27,538.69, and the Dow lost 341 points, or 0.66%, to 51,179.87. Seven of the 11 sectors fell.

Industrials were the worst group by far, down 2.1%. Health care rose 1.1%.

Amgen rose 2.6%, the Dow's best stock. The VIX was 15.08, up 0.5%. Volume was 16.2 billion shares, below the 20-day average of 17.6 billion.

## The number behind it

**One more hike.** The minutes of the Fed's September meeting showed officials projecting another rate increase by the end of 2026, while still debating timing. Some were focused on energy prices and supply-chain pressure, others on whether stronger demand would keep inflation high. Most saw the September quarter-point hike as appropriate, with further moves depending on the data.

The 10-year yield rose 0.8 basis point to 5.28%. Consumer credit rose $8.3 billion in August, below the $13.9 billion forecast.

## What to watch

Thursday's jobless claims and next week's CPI report. With the Fed's path described as data-dependent, that report carries more weight than usual.
