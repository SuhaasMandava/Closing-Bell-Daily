The chip rebound reversed. The Nasdaq Composite fell 0.91% to 25,136.27 and the S&P 500 0.40% to 7,561.67, while the Dow added 0.10% to 52,347.55. Ten of 11 sectors fell, with materials down 1.3% and communication services down 1%. Energy was the only gainer, up 1.5%.

The Philadelphia Semiconductor index fell 3.4%. AMD lost 8.8% and Broadcom 4.8%, and Nvidia fell another 2%.

Decliners beat advancers 1.6 to 1 on the Nasdaq. The VIX rose 3.2% to 19.30. Volume was 16.4 billion shares.

## The number behind it

**0.5%.** Producer prices rose that much in October, according to the Labor Department, as reported by Zacks, adding to the inflation concerns behind a December rate hike. The 10-year yield rose 5.1 basis points to 5.15%.

The market also looked ahead to Anthropic's IPO, expected next week. Zacks said it would test investors' appetite for AI companies and pose challenges for established AI names.

## What to watch

The Anthropic offering and Fed speakers. Nvidia's CEO search remains open.
