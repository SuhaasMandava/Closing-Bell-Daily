Three headlines weighed on stocks. The S&P 500 fell 0.35% to 7,592.40, the Nasdaq 0.39% to 25,366.09 and the Dow 0.36% to 52,297.09. Nine of 11 sectors fell, led by materials, down 1.5%, industrials, down 0.9%, and financials, down 0.7%. Energy gained 1.5%.

Nvidia announced that Jensen Huang was stepping down as president, CEO and board member, effective immediately. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO and Harvey C. Jones becomes the first independent chairman. The company said operations and customer commitments would continue uninterrupted.

The VIX rose 6.9% to 18.70. Nineteen Dow stocks fell.

## The number behind it

**5.10%.** The 10-year yield rose 10.5 basis points to that level. President Trump said in a Truth Social post that Washington no longer needed a deal with Iran, and the U.S. is continuing a blockade on Iranian oil exports. CME FedWatch put the probability of a December hike to 4.00%-4.25% at 89%.

Nvidia fell 4.3% to $166.19.
