The Fed paused and stocks rose for a third straight day. Policymakers kept the target range at 3.75% to 4.00%, but two of the 12 voting members wanted a hike. The S&P 500 gained 0.28% to 7,743.98, the Nasdaq 0.27% to 26,549.85 and the Dow 0.19% to 52,475.10. Six of 11 sectors rose.

Chair Kevin Warsh said additional information would help the committee choose the right stance to ensure "a timely return of inflation to the Committee's 2 percent goal," and that the committee would "deliver price stability." The statement warned that inflation remains elevated.

The 10-year yield rose 5.2 basis points to 5.05%. The VIX fell 3.5% to 16.91.

## The number behind it

**10-2.** The dissents matter more than the hold. They show part of the committee wants to keep raising rates, and they show part of the committee leaning toward more tightening. Earnings were strong. Amphenol's revenue rose 65.1% to $10.23 billion, and Seagate earned $7.68 a share against $7.44 expected.

Oracle rose 10.7% after its investor day, where it set targets for fiscal 2030.

## What to watch
