October ended with another AI selloff. The Nasdaq Composite fell 2.86% to 25,437.00, its worst day in nearly five months, the S&P 500 lost 1.82% to 7,580.07 and the Dow fell 1.09% to 51,770.31. Technology dropped 4.9% and energy rose 1.9%. Six sectors rose, and decliners beat advancers by 3 to 1 on the Nasdaq.

The VIX jumped 12.9% to 19.42. Volume was heavy at 19.9 billion shares.

## What moved

| Sector | Change | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Energy | +1.9% | Oil firm |
| Technology | -4.9% | Chips and software sold off |

Intel plunged 28.6% on a weak fourth-quarter forecast tied to slower PC sales, even though third-quarter earnings of $0.52 a share beat the $0.39 forecast on $16.51 billion in revenue. Amazon fell 1.4% after earnings of $1.89 missed the $2.01 estimate on revenue of $201.94 billion. AMD lost 10.9% and Nvidia 4.2%.

## The number behind it

**-5.3%.** The Nasdaq's loss for October was its worst month since March 2025. The S&P 500 fell 0.9% and the Dow rose 1.7%. For the week the Nasdaq lost 2%, the S&P 500 0.9% and the Dow 0.6%.

Investors also moved into defensive assets ahead of Tuesday's midterm elections.

## What to watch

The November 3 elections, the October jobs report, and Apple's results.
