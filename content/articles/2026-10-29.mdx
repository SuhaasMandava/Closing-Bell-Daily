The earnings season turned. The Nasdaq fell 1.38% to 26,184.72 and the S&P 500 0.30% to 7,720.39, while the Dow lost only 0.25% to 52,342.56 and 23 of its 30 stocks rose. Technology led the losses and nine of 11 sectors still closed higher.

Alphabet fell 12.6% after earnings of $2.12 a share missed the estimate by about 28%, search revenue of $63.4 billion fell short of the $64.9 billion forecast, and free cash flow was negative $9.1 billion against a negative $3.5 billion forecast. ServiceNow fell 27% and Fortinet 20.1%. Alphabet and ServiceNow had their worst single-day performance on record.

The VIX rose 1.7% to 17.20.

## The number behind it

**3.4%.** Third-quarter GDP grew at that annualized rate, above the 3.2% forecast and up from 2.2% in the second quarter. September PCE inflation was 0.5% on the month and 3.6% on the year, with core PCE up 0.3% and 3.1% on the year. Jobless claims were 194,000.

The 10-year yield fell 5.6 basis points to 4.99%.

## What to watch

Amazon's results after the close and the October jobs report next week. Alphabet's search miss raises the question of how much AI chatbots are taking from traditional search.
