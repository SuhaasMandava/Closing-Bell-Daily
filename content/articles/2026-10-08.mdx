A tale of two sectors. The Nasdaq lost 1.25% to 27,193.34 and the S&P 500 fell 0.47% to 7,765.36, while the Dow added 52 points to 51,231.64. Technology fell 1.8% on the day and energy rose 3.0%.

Two stories drove the divergence. A report on OpenAI's revenue hit AI and chip stocks, and escalating Iran tensions pushed crude higher.

## What moved

| Sector | Change | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Energy | +3.0% | Oil surged |
| Technology | -1.8% | AI and chip selloff |

Volume was heavy at 18.8 billion shares against an average of 17.7 billion. On the NYSE there were 110 new highs and 416 new lows. The VIX rose 2.2% to 15.41.

## The number behind it

**$104.28.** Brent settled up 4.07%, and WTI rose 3.64% to $91.49. President Trump said he had no plans to reach a deal merely to end the Iran war, and the U.S. was reportedly planning large-scale strikes. He later said he would not attack Iran before next month's midterm elections, which took some heat out of oil.

The AI item: OpenAI told investors its annualized revenue was about $50 billion at the end of September, below the $68 to $70 billion widely reported earlier, according to market coverage. A person familiar said the higher figure included partners' gross revenue.

The 10-year yield fell 4.6 basis points to 5.23%. Jobless claims were 197,000.

## What to watch

Next week's CPI report. A Brent price above $100 and no deal in sight keep inflation risk elevated.
