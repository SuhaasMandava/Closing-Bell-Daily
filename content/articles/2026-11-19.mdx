The most anticipated IPO of the year did not stop the selloff. The Dow fell 1,101.11 points, or 2.19%, to 49,097.06, its lowest level since June. The S&P 500 lost 2.02% to 7,175.83 and the Nasdaq 2.25% to 23,879.20. All 11 sectors closed lower and decliners beat advancers 4.4 to 1 on the NYSE.

Consumer discretionary fell 2.9%, communication services 2.7%, and health care and materials 2.1% each. The VIX rose 15.8% to 28.44. Volume was 20.8 billion shares.

Anthropic priced at $83 a share, above its $70 to $78 range, and began trading near noon as ANT. Shares rose as much as 34% before the gains faded, and the stock finished up 17% from its first trade at $111.50, according to Zacks. The company briefly passed a $2 trillion market value.

## The number behind it

**$1,101.** That is the Dow's point drop, which left it more than 6% below its November 13 close. The S&P 500 is on track for its steepest weekly loss since April 2025. Jobless claims were 203,000.

The 10-year yield was 5.05%.

## What to watch

Whether the market can stabilize after a week in which the S&P 500 fell more than 5%. Friday's session will show.
