## The number behind it

The ISM Manufacturing PMI came in at 47.2, remaining below the 50 mark that separates contraction from expansion for another straight month.

## What to watch

Friday's Non-Farm Payrolls report will be crucial in deciding whether the Fed cuts rates by 25 or 50 basis points later this month.
