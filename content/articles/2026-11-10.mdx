Inflation stayed high and the Fed sounded hawkish. The S&P 500 fell 0.38% to 7,520.93, the Nasdaq 0.74% to 25,065.87 and the Dow 0.30% to 52,231.89. Six sectors fell and five rose. Technology lost 1.4%, financials and consumer discretionary each gained 0.5%.

The 10-year yield rose 11.9 basis points to 4.98%.

| Sector | Change | Note |

| Consumer Discretionary | +0.5% | Gained |
| Communication Services | -0.2% | Small loss |
| Materials | -0.6% | Weak |
| Technology | -1.4% | Led losses |

The VIX fell 2.3% to 19.46. Volume was 17.1 billion shares.

## The number behind it

**0.4%.** October CPI rose that much on the month, down from 0.6% in September, and core was up 0.3% for a second month. Annual rates for October were not reported because the government shutdown last year left no comparable October 2025 data.

Dallas Fed President Lorie Logan said the Fed may warrant at least two more 25-basis-point hikes. CME FedWatch priced an 87.3% chance of a December hike.

## What to watch

Thursday's session and Friday's PPI. The Fed is expected to raise rates again next month, and the data will show how far.
