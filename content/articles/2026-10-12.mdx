The indexes split. The Dow gained 0.38% to 51,850.55, while the S&P 500 slipped 0.06% to 7,807.24 and the Nasdaq lost 0.44% to 27,245.90. Eight of the 11 sectors rose, but technology fell 0.9% and decliners beat advancers by 1.6 to 1 on the Nasdaq.

Volume was light at 12.7 billion shares against a 20-day average of 17.6 billion.

ConocoPhillips rose 2.6% and Exxon 1.9%. The VIX rose 2.5% to 15.21.

## The number behind it

**$106.65.** Brent settled up $1.93, or 1.8%, its highest close since September 15, and WTI gained 2% to $93.74. A projectile struck Terminal 4 at Riyadh's international airport on Sunday, killing one and injuring 11, a day after a Houthi-claimed attack that killed 13 people and wounded more than 300. President Trump welcomed more Russian diesel exports and a Russia-Ukraine ceasefire covering energy targets, but crude still rose.

Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack said the Fed should tighten "relatively quickly" because inflation is spreading beyond energy. The 10-year yield closed at 5.24%.

## What to watch

Bank earnings begin Tuesday, and the September CPI report arrives Wednesday. Both can reset the rate narrative.
