Weak jobs data, a rare piece of good news for equities this autumn. Nonfarm payrolls rose by 29,000 in September, well below the 79,000 consensus, and July was revised to a loss of 10,000. The unemployment rate rose to 4.2% from 4.1%.

The S&P 500 gained 0.73% to 7,722.72, the Nasdaq 1.19% to 27,190.86, and the Dow 0.49% to 51,176.96. Ten of 11 sectors rose. For the week, the S&P 500 fell 0.3%, the Nasdaq rose 0.5% and the Dow fell 1.3%.

| Health Care | -0.04% | Only decliner |

Cisco was the Dow's best stock at +3.6%. The VIX fell 6.6% to 15.31.

## The number behind it

**77.9%.** After the report, CME FedWatch put that probability on the Fed holding at 3.75% to 4.00% in October, a sharp swing from the 71% odds of a hike that markets priced on September 24. Average hourly earnings rose 0.1% on the month and 3% from a year earlier.

The 10-year yield ended at 5.28%, up 4 basis points, so the bond market did not fully join the rally.

## What to watch

The September CPI report and next week's Fed minutes. The case for stagflation, weak jobs with 3.4% inflation, is the main bearish argument now.
