Money moved from growth to value. The Dow rose 1.08% to 52,177.69, the S&P 500 gained 0.33% to 7,744.06 and the Nasdaq edged up 0.08% to 26,713.03. Advancers beat decliners 3.1 to 1 on the S&P 500, and 23 Dow stocks rose.

The 10-year yield fell 10.1 basis points to 5.09%, a drop of more than 10 basis points in a single day.

## What moved

| Sector | Change | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Health Care | +1.8% | Led the S&P 500 |
| Materials | +1.7% | Cyclicals bounced |
| Financials | +1.4% | Banks recovered |
| Energy | -1.6% | Only major decliner |

Eli Lilly rose 3.6% and Newmont 3.8%. Volume was heavy at 20.7 billion shares. The VIX fell 2.4% to 16.46.

## The number behind it

**5.09%.** The fall in yield took some pressure off, but the rotation was driven more by what investors were avoiding: debt-sensitive technology and AI shares. Zacks characterized it as selling in AI names and buying of defensive and undervalued sectors.

Jobless claims were 199,000 for the week ended October 10, a rise of 1,000.

## What to watch

Friday's industrial production and the question of whether the value rotation continues. The 10-year at 5.09% is still above where the month began.
