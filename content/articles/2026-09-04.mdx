Stocks finished lower on Friday after the U.S. added 162,000 jobs in August — roughly three times the pace economists expected — a print strong enough to put a Federal Reserve rate hike back on the table for this month's meeting. The Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 271.86 points (0.51%) to 53,414.25, the S&P 500 slid 0.38% to 7,718.60, and the Nasdaq Composite dropped 0.29% to 26,506.99.

## The number behind it

162,000 — the number of jobs added in August, blowing past consensus estimates of roughly 53,000-55,000. The unemployment rate held steady at 4.1%, and prior months' figures were revised higher, giving the Fed more room to consider tightening.

## What to watch

Next week's CPI and PPI reports, which economists say will be the deciding factor for whether the Fed hikes or holds at its September 16-17 meeting.
