Markets extended their bounce on Thursday, marking a second straight day of gains. The S&P 500 jumped 1.06% to close at 7,747.71, the Nasdaq Composite surged 1.4% to 26,584.06, and the Dow Jones Industrial Average advanced 624.16 points (1.18%) to 53,686.11. Long-dated Treasury yields eased further and the dollar weakened, giving risk assets room to run.

## The number behind it

99 — the level the U.S. Dollar Index dropped below on Thursday, its weakest since late August. A softer dollar tends to lift multinational earnings and risk appetite, and it lined up with a broader pullback in yields to fuel the day's rally.

## What to watch

Friday's August jobs report, which traders expect to reset expectations heading into the Fed's September meeting.
