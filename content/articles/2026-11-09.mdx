The biggest up day of the autumn. The S&P 500 rose 2.35% to 7,549.94, the Nasdaq 2.74% to 25,253.14 and the Dow 2.15% to 52,389.75. All 11 sectors rose, and 25 of 30 Dow stocks advanced. The VIX fell 22.3% to 19.92.

Industrials gained 3.8%, consumer discretionary 3.1% and technology 2.9%.

## What moved

| Sector | Change | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Industrials | +3.8% | Best sector |
| Consumer Discretionary | +3.1% | Lower oil helped |
| Technology | +2.9% | Chips rebounded |

Volume was 19 billion shares. Constellation Energy reported earnings of $5.06 a share against $3.90 expected, on revenue of $9.55 billion.

## The number behind it

**$85.93.** WTI fell 1.1% to that level and Brent 0.9% to $91.75, a long way below the $104.96 Brent settled at a week earlier. Falling oil eased inflation worries ahead of this week's CPI report, which supported the market.

Nvidia stayed in focus. Its board launched an independent investigation after a New York Times report about its CEO, Jensen Huang. The company said it had no reason to believe that U.S. national security or company secrets had been compromised, and Huang said he supported the probe. The 10-year yield was 4.86%.

## What to watch

Tuesday's October CPI report. A hot number would test a rally built on cheaper oil.
