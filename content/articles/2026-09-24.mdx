The close hid a rough session. The S&P 500 slipped 0.02% to 7,704.13, the Nasdaq rose 0.01% to 26,939.37, and the Dow fell 162 points to 51,349.98, its third straight loss. Stocks came off their lows after a report that U.S. and Iranian negotiators were meeting in New York and considering a phased deal.

The bond market was the main story. The 10-year yield rose as high as 5.223% and closed at 5.16%, the highest since June 2007. The 30-year touched 5.501%, its highest since June 2004.

Eight of the 11 sectors fell. The VIX rose 3.2% to 15.67.

## The number behind it

**71%.** That is the CME FedWatch probability of another Fed hike in October, up from 66% a day earlier and from 8.8% a month ago. The reported deal framework would have the U.S. lift sanctions on Iran in exchange for reopening the Strait of Hormuz, but oil did not wait for it: Brent rose more than 3% to near $107 and WTI 2.7% to $94.61.

## What to watch

The U.S.-China summit's outcome and whether the Iran talks produce an announcement. The market has two competing stories, a possible deal and a rising rate path, and yields are currently winning.
