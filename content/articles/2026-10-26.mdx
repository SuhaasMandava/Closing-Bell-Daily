The AI trade got two pieces of news over the weekend and stocks liked both. The S&P 500 rose 0.70% to 7,676.77, the Nasdaq 0.82% to 26,169.38 and the Dow 0.28% to 52,361.58. Nine of 11 sectors advanced. At its high the Dow was up nearly 405 points.

Anthropic filed a Form S-1 with the SEC for a planned Nasdaq listing under the ticker ANT, reporting revenue of $16.29 billion for the first half of 2026, up from $1.16 billion a year earlier. Meta said it would raise $95 billion to fund AI infrastructure, starting with a $35 billion offering of common and mandatory convertible preferred stock.

SpaceX said it completed a $40 billion debt financing to pay for AI chips. Verizon fell 3.5% after revenue of $34.31 billion missed the $34.81 billion estimate. The VIX fell 5.5% to 18.39.

## The number behind it

**$95 billion.** Meta's plan is one of the largest corporate financings tied to AI, and its structure, with $22.5 billion of Class A common stock and $12.5 billion of preferred in the first tranche, shows that equity, not just debt, is paying for the buildout. The 10-year yield was 4.99%.
