Tech sold off hard to close the week. The Nasdaq Composite fell 2.15% to 26,140.03, the S&P 500 lost 1.09% to 7,659.44 and the Dow fell 175 points to 52,002.41. Technology and communication services each dropped 2.5%, and energy rose 2.5%.

The VIX jumped 21.5% to 19.99, its highest close in this stretch.

## What moved

| Sector | Change | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Energy | +2.5% | Brent rose 3.7% |
| Consumer Discretionary | -1.2% | Weak |
| Technology | -2.5% | Chips led the drop |
| Communication Services | -2.5% | Mega-cap weakness |

Six sectors rose and five fell. The Philadelphia Semiconductor index lost about 3.8%, its eighth straight decline, according to market coverage. For the week, the Nasdaq fell 4.5%, the S&P 500 1.9% and the Dow rose 0.7%.

## The number behind it

**$109.86.** Brent rose 3.7% to that level, and WTI 2.6% to $99.02. Chevron rose 2.6%. Oil, inflation and AI financing worries all weighed on tech at once. September industrial production rose 0.7%, above the 0.4% forecast, adding to the case that demand remains strong enough to keep rates high.

## What to watch

Third-quarter earnings from the big tech companies, and whether the VIX stays near 20.
