The inflation report was soft and the market did not care. The S&P 500 fell 0.25% to 7,651.54 and the Dow lost 444 points, or 0.86%, to 50,906.05, while the Nasdaq edged up 0.24% to 26,861.06 on strength in AI-linked software. Seven of 11 sectors fell.

For the quarter, the S&P 500 gained about 2.0% and the Nasdaq about 2.5%, while the Dow lost about 2.7%. For September, the Dow lost about 4.3% and the S&P 500 about 0.5%.

Synopsys rose 4.8%. The VIX closed at 16.34, up 1.9%. Volume was 18.1 billion shares against a 20-day average of 17.1 billion.

## The number behind it

**0.2%.** Core PCE prices rose that much in August, below the 0.3% forecast, and July was revised down to 0.1%. Headline PCE rose 0.3% against an expected 0.4%. On a year-over-year basis, headline PCE was 3.4% and core 3.0%, still well above the Fed's 2% target. Personal income rose 0.2%, short of the 0.5% forecast.

The bond market did not respond to the softer reading. The 10-year touched 5.3% during the session and closed at 5.29%, with the 30-year reaching 5.642%, its highest since 2002.

## What to watch

Friday's September payrolls report. A weak number would test whether the bond market is reacting to data at all or only to supply and the oil shock.
