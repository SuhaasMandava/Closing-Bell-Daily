October opened with a recovery from the lows. The S&P 500 gained 0.19% to 7,666.45, the Nasdaq edged up 0.04% to 26,871.60, and the Dow rose 0.04% to 50,926.56. At the lows, the S&P 500 had been down about 0.5% and the Dow nearly 360 points.

The turning point was the bond market. The 10-year yield hit 5.344% the day before, its highest since April 2002, and then fell about 6 basis points in late trading. It closed at 5.24%.

The VIX was 16.39. Advancers outnumbered decliners by 1.2 to 1 on the NYSE, volume was 17.25 billion shares.

## The number behind it

**$54.23 billion.** That is Micron's fiscal fourth-quarter revenue, up 379.3% from a year earlier and 6.3% above estimates. Non-GAAP earnings were $33.42 per share against $3.03 a year earlier. Data center SSD revenue approached $10 billion, more than 10 times the year-ago level. The company cited tight DRAM and NAND supply and AI demand for the pricing.

ISM manufacturing for September fell marginally to 54.5.

## What to watch

Friday's payrolls. After a month of rate-driven selling, the labor data will show whether the Fed has more reason to hike again.
