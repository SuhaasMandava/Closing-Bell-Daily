A rebound, but a narrow one. The Nasdaq Composite rose 1.11% to 26,430.89 and the S&P 500 gained 0.41% to 7,690.73, while the Dow fell 36 points to 51,966.03. Only four of 11 sectors rose. Consumer discretionary led with a 2.7% gain, technology rose 1.2%, and real estate fell 1.2%.

The semiconductor ETF SOXX rose about 2.4% after an 11.8% drop the week before, its worst week since March, according to market coverage.

| Sector | Change | Note |

| Real Estate | -1.2% | Worst sector |

JPMorgan fell 1.8%, the Dow's worst stock, and 18 of 30 Dow components fell. The VIX dropped 7.75% to 18.44, and volume was light at 15.3 billion shares.

## The number behind it

**$109.96.** Brent rose 0.37% to that level and WTI 0.27% to $98.81. An earlier drop in crude had assumed that Russia-related measures would ease supply, but attacks on Saudi energy infrastructure renewed worries about Middle East supply. The 10-year yield ended at 5.14%, up 3.8 basis points.

## What to watch

The week brings third-quarter results from large technology companies, including Meta, and the Fed meeting on Wednesday next week. Oil above $100 keeps inflation on the table.
