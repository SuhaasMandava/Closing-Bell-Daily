A cautious bounce. The Nasdaq Composite rose 0.83% to 25,372.01 and the S&P 500 0.36% to 7,568.92, while the Dow fell 197 points, or 0.38%, to 51,786.72. Only four of 11 sectors rose.

Technology gained 1.3%, industrials 1.0% and communication services 0.9%, while materials lost 1.9%. Decliners beat advancers 1.6 to 1 on the S&P 500.

## What moved

| Sector | Change | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technology | +1.3% | Semiconductors rebounded |
| Industrials | +1.0% | Steady |
| Communication Services | +0.9% | Mega-cap gains |
| Materials | -1.9% | Worst sector |

The Philadelphia Semiconductor index rose 3%, with Broadcom up 3.1% and Nvidia 2.3%. Zacks described the move as bargain hunting after the Nasdaq-100 fell into a correction on Monday. The VIX fell 1.5% to 20.83 and volume was 16.6 billion shares.

## The number behind it

**1.8%.** Factory orders rose that much in September against a 1.1% forecast, and August was revised up to 0.3%. The 10-year yield rose 5.8 basis points to 4.95%.

Investors largely avoided election bets. The Democrats' chances of taking the House were a source of uncertainty for AI and data-center companies because of possible oversight.

## What to watch

The election results, which will arrive overnight. Markets will focus on the House and Senate balance and on any policy signals for technology.
