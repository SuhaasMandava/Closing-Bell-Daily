It was the worst day of the year for tech. The Nasdaq Composite plunged 5.52%, or 1,421.52 points, to 24,315.00, its steepest one-day drop in 19 months. The S&P 500 lost 4.36% to 7,353.73, about $3 trillion in market value, and the Dow fell 1,833 points, or 3.44%, to 51,523.06. All 30 Dow stocks fell and all 11 sectors closed lower. Decliners beat advancers by 6.1 to 1.

The Philadelphia Semiconductor index closed down about 12%. Volume was 26.9 billion shares, against an average of 17.7 billion.

Nvidia fell 9.3% and GE Vernova 7.5%. The VIX jumped 69.7% to 28.18.

## The number behind it

**12%.** The semiconductor index's decline came after Democratic wins in states where opposition to new data centers has grown, raising fears that policy changes could slow the AI buildout behind this year's rally. Coverage described the day as one of the worst for chip stocks on record. The Nasdaq also closed more than 10% below its October 6 high.

Brent rose more than $3 after new tanker attacks in the Gulf. The 10-year yield fell 5.7 basis points to 4.84%.

## What to watch

Friday's jobs report, and whether Thursday's decline was a liquidation or the start of a repricing. The VIX above 28 is the highest in this period.
