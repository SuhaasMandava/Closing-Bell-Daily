Equities spent the session in a narrow range, with volume running roughly 22%
below the one-month average as desks thinned out ahead of tomorrow's inflation
print. The S&P's entire intraday range was under 0.6% — the tightest of the
month.

## What moved

| Sector | Change | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Energy | +0.7% | Only sector to close green |
| Utilities | -0.1% | Effectively flat |
| Semiconductors | -1.2% | Gave back part of last week's rally |
| Consumer discretionary | -0.8% | Retail soft ahead of earnings |

Energy got a modest bid from crude, which finished up a little over a dollar.
Nothing in the tape suggested anyone wanted a position on before the data.

## What to watch

Tomorrow's CPI release lands before the open. Consensus is +0.2% on the
monthly core reading. A hotter number puts the September rate-cut debate back
on the table and likely ends the low-volatility stretch the market has been
enjoying since late July.
