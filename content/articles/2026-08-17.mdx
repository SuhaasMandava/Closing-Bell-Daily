Stocks fell for a second straight session on Monday, extending Friday's pullback, as oil prices jumped and long-dated Treasury yields climbed to levels not seen in nearly two decades — overshadowing another burst of enthusiasm around the AI trade.

## The number behind it

The 30-year Treasury yield touched its highest level since 2007, as traders priced in the inflationary risk of Brent crude pushing toward $90 a barrel. The move followed the lapse of a US-Iran memorandum of understanding, reviving fears that the two countries' conflict could disrupt the Strait of Hormuz — a critical corridor for global oil supply.

## What to watch

Retail earnings take center stage this week, with Target and Walmart set to report before Nvidia's closely watched results the following week. Also on deck: July housing starts and industrial production data, plus any further signals on where the Iran standoff — and oil prices — head next.
