It was a narrow loss with a wide miss underneath. The S&P 500 fell 0.77% to 7,683.69, the Nasdaq lost 0.92% to 26,820.38, and the Dow dropped 347 points to 51,481.51. Chip designer Arm plunged 8.7%, the worst performer on the Nasdaq.

Most sectors held up. Industrials rose 1%, while communication services and energy each fell 0.9%. Decliners beat advancers by 3.55 to 1 on the NYSE, a sign that the index loss was spread more widely than the sector tally implies.

Intraday, the S&P 500 was down about 1% at its low and the Dow was down nearly 419 points. The VIX rose 8.1% to 16.07.

## The number behind it

**5.24%.** The 10-year yield closed there, up 5.6 basis points and the highest close since June 2007. The 30-year touched 5.552%, a level last seen in 2004.

Oil stayed high because the Iran standoff showed no sign of ending. President Trump reportedly rejected a peace proposal that would have reopened the Strait of Hormuz, though reports later in the day said he might reconsider sanctions relief, and Qatari mediators were set to talk with both sides.

## What to watch

Tuesday's job openings report and consumer confidence data, then Friday's payrolls. The Fed has said it may hike again this year, and the bond market is already pricing in a lot of tightening.
