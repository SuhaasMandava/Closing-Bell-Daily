A hot inflation report turned a quiet start into a sharp tech selloff. The Nasdaq lost 1.88% to 26,692.87, the S&P 500 fell 1.11% to 7,718.94 and the Dow slipped just 0.09% to 51,621.48. Eight of 11 sectors fell, and the VIX rose 10.5% to 16.87.

Indexes gave up early gains after the report.

## What moved

| Sector | Change | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technology | -2.6% | Led the decline |
| Communication Services | -1.7% | Mega-cap tech weak |
| Consumer Discretionary | -1.1% | Amazon fell 1.7% |

Nvidia fell 3.2%. Seventeen Dow components rose, a sign that the damage was concentrated in growth names. Volume reached 18 billion shares.

## The number behind it

**3.7%.** That was headline CPI inflation in September, above the 3.6% forecast, with core at 2.6% against 2.5% expected. Prices rose 0.6% on the month, up from 0.4% in August, with core up 0.3%.

The 10-year yield ended at 5.19%, down 2.7 basis points, so the equity damage came from repricing the Fed rather than from rising yields. Vice Chair for Supervision Michelle Bowman said the Fed could wait to assess its September hike before deciding on further action.

## What to watch

Thursday's jobless claims and the debt-heavy AI names, which fell hardest. If yields stay near 5.2%, valuations remain the pressure point.
