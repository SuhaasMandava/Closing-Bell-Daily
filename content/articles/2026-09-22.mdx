A quiet index result hid a lopsided day. The Nasdaq added 0.45% to 27,244.28, a second straight record. The S&P 500 was unchanged at 7,764.64, and the Dow fell 185 points to 51,863.69. Six of the 11 sectors rose.

Financials took the heaviest hit, dropping 2%, while consumer staples gained 1% and technology 0.7%.

## What moved

| Sector | Change | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Consumer Staples | +1.0% | Defensive buying |
| Technology | +0.7% | AI names kept rising |
| Financials | -2.0% | Worst sector by a wide margin |

Sandisk rose 6.8%. The VIX fell 4.4% to 14.21. Volume was 17.9 billion shares against a 20-day average of 16.3 billion.

## The number behind it

**$99.25.** Brent crude settled below $100 after a 1.1% drop, its fifth straight decline, and WTI fell 1.2% to $94.59. President Trump said officials had a "very good meeting" with an Iranian delegation that ran three hours. Oil eased earlier on a report that Iran was ready to open the Strait of Hormuz in seven days.

The tone was not all calm. Speaking at the UN, Trump said he had to make a "big decision" on whether to extend talks or "annihilate" Iran.

## What to watch

The U.S.-China summit on Thursday, where investors hope for an extension of the trade truce and possibly a discussion of AI regulation. The 10-year yield, at 4.97%, remains the market's main sensitivity.
