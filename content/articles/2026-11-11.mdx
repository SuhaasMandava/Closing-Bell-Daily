Tech led the way back. The S&P 500 gained 1.31% to 7,619.38, the Nasdaq 1.60% to 25,466.19 and the Dow 0.49% to 52,486.49. Ten of 11 sectors rose. Technology gained 2.7%, energy 1.8% and industrials 1.3%, while consumer discretionary fell 0.4%.

The VIX fell 10.1% to 17.49. Volume was light at 14.7 billion shares against an average of 18.7 billion.

Apple rose 2% to a record close of $355.63, beating the prior peak of $355.38 from November 5, with a market value of about $5.1 trillion. Seventeen S&P 500 stocks hit new highs and none hit a new low.

## The number behind it

**$355.63.** Apple's close is a record, and it has held up through a month when the Nasdaq entered a correction. It shows how selective the market has been: the rotation away from AI infrastructure has not been a rotation away from the largest platform companies.

The 10-year yield rose 1.5 basis points to 5.00%.

Nvidia's investigation and Friday's PPI.
