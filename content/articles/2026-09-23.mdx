The relief rally ended quickly. The S&P 500 fell 0.75% to 7,706.03, the Nasdaq lost 1.13% to 26,936.04, and the Dow shed 352 points to 51,511.59. Ten of 11 sectors closed lower, and decliners beat advancers by 1.9 to 1.

Yields were the main pressure. The 10-year yield rose to 5.135% intraday, its highest since July 2007, and closed at 5.11%. The 2-year yield hit 4.947%, its highest since May 2024.

Oracle fell 3.1%, Broadcom 2.6% and Nvidia 1.5%. The VIX rose 6.8% to 15.18.

## The number behind it

**$103.08.** Brent jumped 3.9% to that level after Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian told the UN that Iran would not surrender to the U.S. "under pressure." The remark came a day after President Trump described three hours of talks as a "good meeting." WTI rose 1.8% to $92.16.

Fed Governor Michael Barr added to the rate pressure, saying further policy adjustments would be needed to bring down inflation while growth is robust and the labor market is solid. CME FedWatch put the odds of an October hike at 66%.

## What to watch

Thursday's U.S.-China summit, weekly jobless claims and any further signs on Iran. The 10-year is trading at levels not seen in 19 years, and each new high tests how much the equity market can absorb.
