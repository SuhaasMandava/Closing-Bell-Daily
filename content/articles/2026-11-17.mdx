There was nowhere to hide. All 11 sectors closed lower. The Dow fell 848.75 points, or 1.64%, to 50,759.42, the S&P 500 1.03% to 7,340.53 and the Nasdaq 0.74% to 24,328.38. Decliners beat advancers 3.8 to 1 on the NYSE, which recorded 702 new 52-week lows.

The VIX rose 11.1% to 25.46.

## What moved

| Sector | Change | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technology | -0.9% | Smaller than Monday |
| Utilities | -1.6% | Defensives fell too |
| Financials | -1.9% | Worst sector |

Intuit dropped 7.2%. The market also reacted to a network outage at the National Security Agency, which raised doubts about the nation's tech infrastructure. The agency said it had experienced temporary degradation during scheduled maintenance, and the government said there was no evidence of a cyberattack.

## The number behind it

**0.2%.** Retail sales rose that much in October, in line with forecasts. The data was not the driver. The market was focused on Nvidia's report after the close and on Anthropic's pricing, expected Wednesday. The 10-year yield fell 1.8 basis points to 5.10%.

## What to watch

Nvidia's results, Anthropic's IPO pricing, and the Fed minutes on Wednesday.
