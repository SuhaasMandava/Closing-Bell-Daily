Another small decline, another high in yields. The S&P 500 fell 0.17% to 7,670.84, the Nasdaq lost 0.09% to 26,797.54, and the Dow fell 132 points to 51,349.92. It was the second straight loss for all three indexes.

The 10-year yield briefly touched 5.253%, a 19-year high, and closed at 5.26% even as oil fell and the data weakened.

Decliners beat advancers by 1.7 to 1 on the NYSE. The VIX was 16.04, little changed. Volume was 16.15 billion shares.

## The number behind it

**81.9.** The Conference Board's consumer confidence index for September missed the 89.1 consensus by a wide margin and was the lowest in 12.5 months. The present-situation component fell to 109.3 from 117.2.

The labor figures were mixed. August job openings fell by 256,000 to 7.079 million, but hiring rose 46,000 to 5.192 million and layoffs fell 61,000 to 1.641 million. The 30-year yield reached 5.6%, a level last seen in 2002.

## What to watch

Wednesday's PCE inflation report, the Fed's preferred gauge, and the end of the quarter. Bad news on growth is not lowering yields, which suggests the bond market is still focused on inflation and supply.
