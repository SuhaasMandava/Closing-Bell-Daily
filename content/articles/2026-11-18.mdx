A fifth straight loss for the S&P 500. The index slipped 0.23% to 7,323.63 and the Dow lost 561.25 points, or 1.11%, to 50,198.17, while the Nasdaq gained 0.42% to 24,429.88. Six sectors fell. Energy dropped 3.3% and health care 2.2%.

UnitedHealth plunged 10.2%, a major drag on the Dow.

The VIX fell 3.5% to 24.56. Volume was 17.5 billion shares.

## The number behind it

**$115.84 billion.** That was Nvidia's revenue for the quarter ended October, which topped estimates by 7.07%, and earnings were $2.68 a share against $2.47 expected. The company raised its revenue forecast, but the stock still fell 2.9%.

Housing starts rose 9.9% in October to an annual rate of 1.309 million, while permits fell 7.4% to 1.295 million. Pending home sales fell 4.7%. The 10-year yield fell 3.7 basis points to 5.07%.
