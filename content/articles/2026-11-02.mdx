Oil fell hard and tech fell harder. Brent crude dropped $8.47, or 7.47%, to $104.96, and WTI fell 7.2% to $96.62, after Iran signaled it was open to peace talks with the United States following eight months of conflict. Energy fell 4%.

The Dow rose 0.41% to 51,983.98, but the S&P 500 lost 0.51% to 7,541.40 and the Nasdaq 1.07% to 25,164.31. The Nasdaq-100 closed more than 10% below its October 6 record, a technical correction.

Seven of 11 sectors rose and advancers beat decliners by 2.2 to 1 on the S&P 500. Equinor fell 6.9% and Exxon 4.1%. Apple lost 1.8% ahead of its earnings. The VIX rose 8.9% to 21.15, its highest close in this stretch.

## The number behind it

**$104.96.** Brent's close is still above $100, but the one-day drop is a very large move and shows how much geopolitical risk was in the price. Cheaper oil should ease inflation worries, though Fed officials have said inflation is spreading beyond energy.

## What to watch

Tuesday's midterm elections and Friday's jobs report. If oil keeps falling the rate outlook improves, but tech is dealing with a valuation reset of its own.
