A partial recovery. The Nasdaq Composite rose 1.09% to 24,579.43 and the S&P 500 0.31% to 7,376.86, while the Dow fell 237 points, or 0.46%, to 51,285.73. Seven of 11 sectors fell. Technology gained 1.3%.

The VIX fell 9% to 25.64, still well above 20.

## What moved

| Sector | Change | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technology | +1.3% | Chips rebounded |
| Communication Services | +0.5% | Small gain |
| Industrials | +0.1% | Flat |
| Consumer Staples | -1.2% | Weakest |

Twenty-two Dow stocks fell. Volume was 16.9 billion shares, below the 20-day average.

## The number behind it

**165,000.** Nonfarm payrolls rose that much in October, and September was revised up to 60,000 from 29,000. The unemployment rate held at 4.1% and average hourly earnings rose 0.4% on the month and 3.1% on the year.

After the report, CME FedWatch showed a 76.3% probability that the Fed would raise rates to 4.00%-4.25%. The 10-year yield rose 2.2 basis points to 4.86%.

## What to watch

October CPI next week and the Fed's path. With the labor market firm, oil falling and the market volatile, inflation data will decide whether the hike is priced.
