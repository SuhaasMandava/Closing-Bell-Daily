Broad gains, with earnings doing the work. The S&P 500 rose 0.58% to 7,757.17, the Nasdaq 1.05% to 26,702.86 and the Dow 0.69% to 52,856.09. Ten of 11 sectors advanced.

Super Micro raised its first-quarter revenue outlook to $18.5 to $19.5 billion from $14.5 to $15.5 billion and its gross margin forecast to 12% to 12.5% from 10.4% to 10.8%. The stock jumped 9.6%.

Dell rose 5.3% and Hewlett Packard Enterprise 3.7%. SpaceX reported third-quarter earnings of $0.35 a share on $14.57 billion in revenue, up 161%, and rose 7.8%. Elon Musk said SpaceX and Tesla formed special committees to discuss a merger, and Tesla gained 3.4%.

## The number behind it

**$18.5 to $19.5 billion.** That is Super Micro's new revenue range for its first fiscal quarter of 2027, nearly $4 billion above the earlier forecast, and it is a direct read on AI server orders. The 10-year yield held at about 5.12% and the VIX fell 3.6% to 16.68.

## What to watch

Meta reports Wednesday after the close. Its capital spending plans will tell investors whether AI server demand is accelerating.
