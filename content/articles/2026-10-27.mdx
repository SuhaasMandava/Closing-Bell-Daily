Everything lined up for a second day of gains. The S&P 500 rose 0.60% to 7,722.60, the Nasdaq 1.18% to 26,477.81 and the Dow 0.03% to 52,377.57. Technology gained 1.7% and energy lost 2.1%.

The Philadelphia Semiconductor index rose 3.6% and the SOXX ETF 3.9%.

## What moved

| Sector | Change | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technology | +1.7% | Chips surged |
| Energy | -2.1% | Oil fell |

Six of 11 sectors fell even as the index rose, and 19 of the 30 Dow stocks declined. Advancers beat decliners by 1.8 to 1 on the NYSE. The VIX fell 4.7% to 17.52.

## The number behind it

**91.7.** The Conference Board's consumer confidence index for October came in well above the 82.8 forecast, up from a revised 84.2 in September. The present-situation index rose to 120.4 from 111.8.

Oil fell after reports that Iran's supreme leader, Mojtaba Khamenei, and security chief Mohsen Rezaei were considering ways to end the war with the U.S. and Israel. WTI fell 1.83% to $99.90 and Brent 1.67% to $113.88. The 10-year yield was 4.99%.

## What to watch

Wednesday's Fed decision. With confidence higher and oil lower, the question is whether policymakers see room to hold.
