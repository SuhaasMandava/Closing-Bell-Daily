The week opened with a broad selloff. The S&P 500 fell 1.92% to 7,416.59, the Nasdaq 2.49% to 24,509.76 and the Dow 1.41%, or 739 points, to 51,608.17. Nine of 11 sectors fell, technology by 3.2%, while consumer staples rose 0.3%. Decliners beat advancers 3.5 to 1 on the NYSE.

The VIX jumped 18.7% to 22.91. The NYSE logged 590 new 52-week lows and 68 highs.

## What moved

| Sector | Change | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Consumer Staples | +0.3% | Top sector |
| Technology | -3.2% | Chips led |

The Philadelphia Semiconductor index fell 5.3%. Intel dropped 7% and Micron 7.6%. The index was up 106.6% for the year in mid-June and is now up about 43%.

## The number behind it

**5.3%.** The one-day decline in the chip index followed reports that Nvidia's board has no plans to reinstate Jensen Huang, who resigned as CEO last week, and a new Trump administration rule restricting China's access to AI chips from major U.S. tech companies. Zacks said the rule could weigh on chipmakers' fortunes.

The 10-year yield was 5.12%.

## What to watch

Nvidia reports after the close on Tuesday, Anthropic's IPO is expected this week, and the Fed's minutes are due Wednesday.
