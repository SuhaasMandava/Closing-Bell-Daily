Industrial earnings did the lifting. The Dow gained 1.02% to 52,495.88, with 22 of its 30 stocks higher, and the S&P 500 rose 0.28% to 7,712.57. The Nasdaq Composite was flat, down 0.02% to 26,424.82, after swinging between a 250-point loss and a 171-point gain.

Eight of 11 sectors fell even though the S&P rose. Consumer discretionary gained 1.8% and technology 1.4%, while utilities lost 2% and real estate 1.2%.

## What moved

| Sector | Change | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Consumer Discretionary | +1.8% | Led the market |
| Technology | +1.4% | Held gains |
| Real Estate | -1.2% | Rate-sensitive |
| Utilities | -2.0% | Worst sector |

The VIX fell 6.1% to 17.31, and volume was 15.9 billion shares.

## The number behind it

**$2.50.** That was 3M's adjusted third-quarter earnings per share against a $2.40 estimate, on revenue of $6.6 billion, up 4.2% from a year earlier. The stock rose 5.8%. Northrop Grumman earned $7.89 a share against $7.22 expected, with sales of $11.12 billion up 6.7%, and rose 4.6%.

Oil fell as investors tracked the Middle East. WTI declined 1.8% to $101.79 and Brent 0.7% to $108.79. The 10-year yield was 5.12%.

## What to watch

Meta and other big technology reports later this week, then the Fed on Wednesday next week.
