Stocks closed the week firmly. The S&P 500 gained 0.59% to 7,811.54, the Dow rose 423 points, or 0.83%, to 51,654.95, and the Nasdaq added 0.64% to 27,366.17. Nine of the 11 sectors rose, and 22 of 30 Dow stocks advanced.

For the week, the S&P 500 gained about 1.1%, the Dow 0.9% and the Nasdaq 0.6%.

## What moved

| Sector | Change | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Real Estate | +1.9% | Crown Castle and American Tower each rose over 15% |
| Consumer Discretionary | +1.7% | Easing worries |
| Health Care | +1.6% | Defensives joined |
| Communication Services | -0.4% | Only decliner |

The VIX fell 3.7% to 14.84, and volume was light at 14.4 billion shares against an average of 17.8 billion. The Roundhill Magnificent Seven ETF rose 1%.

## The number behind it

**15.6%.** That was Crown Castle's gain, with American Tower up 15.5%, on optimism about satellite connectivity and wireless infrastructure. The broader driver was President Trump's statement that the U.S. would not attack Iran before the November midterms, citing productive talks.

Oil still rose 0.4%, with WTI at $91.85 and Brent at $104.72, as shipping risks offset the political relief. The 10-year yield was 5.24%.

## What to watch

Third-quarter earnings season begins next week with the large banks, and fresh inflation data is due. Both will show whether the rally has earnings support.
