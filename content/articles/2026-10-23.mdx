Oil took over. The Dow lost 652.82 points, or 1.23%, to 52,215.16, the S&P 500 fell 0.52% to 7,623.68 and the Nasdaq slipped 0.21% to 25,955.34. At its low the Dow was down nearly 813 points. Eight of 11 sectors fell, and 24 of 30 Dow stocks declined.

Cyclicals suffered the most. Consumer staples fell 1.9% while energy rose 2.6%.

## What moved

| Sector | Change | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Energy | +2.6% | Oil surged |
| Consumer Staples | -1.9% | Worst sector |

American Express fell 3.7% after it said it would cut about 5% of its global workforce to prepare for AI. Caterpillar lost 2.4%. The VIX rose 2.2% to 19.45.

## The number behind it

**$116.72.** Brent settled up 3.1% at that level, a five-month high, after reports that the U.S. and Israel could be planning another attack on Iran. WTI rose 3.8% to $103.27. Iran said it would retaliate against any aggression.

The University of Michigan's final October sentiment reading was 48.0, up from 46.3 in the preliminary data. One-year inflation expectations rose to 4.7% and five-year to 3.5%.

For the week, the S&P 500 fell 0.5%, the Nasdaq 0.7% and the Dow rose 0.4%.

## What to watch

Next week's Fed meeting, more earnings from big technology companies, and any news on Iran that moves Brent.
