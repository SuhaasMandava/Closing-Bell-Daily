Stocks bounced back on Wednesday, with all three major benchmarks snapping a three-day losing streak. The Dow Jones Industrial Average added 295.07 points (0.56%) to close at 53,061.95, the S&P 500 rose 0.46% to 7,666.60, and the Nasdaq Composite gained 0.45% to 26,217.83. The relief came as Treasury yields, which had been climbing toward multiyear highs, finally paused.

## The number behind it

4.79% — roughly where the 10-year Treasury yield sat as the recent climb toward multiyear highs stalled out. Stocks had been under pressure for days as rising yields and elevated oil prices stoked inflation worries; a pause in that climb was enough to bring buyers back in.

## What to watch

Whether the pullback in yields holds, and how much weight the Fed puts on a September rate hike after a Fed official pushed back on the idea that one is a lock.
