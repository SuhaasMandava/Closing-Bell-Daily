Tech led the market down. The S&P 500 fell 0.48% to 7,619.98, the Nasdaq lost 0.56% to 26,186.41, and the Dow dropped 152 points to 52,421.20. The damage was concentrated in AI-linked stocks after executives at Anthropic, OpenAI and xAI cautioned about the risks of the technology's pace, and in rate-sensitive sectors as Treasury yields climbed.

The index losses were small. The split underneath them was not: the technology sector fell 1.7% while communication services rose 2.8%.

## What moved

| Sector | Change | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Communication Services | +2.8% | Only large gainer |
| Utilities | -1.3% | Hit by higher yields |
| Industrials | -1.4% | Oil and rate pressure |
| Technology | -1.7% | Chipmakers led the drop |

Eight of the 11 sectors closed lower. Nvidia fell 3.4% and Broadcom 4.8%. The VIX rose 8% to 17.10. Volume was 15.3 billion shares against a 20-day average of 14.8 billion.

## The number behind it

**5.01%.** The 10-year Treasury yield briefly crossed 5% during the session, its highest level since October 2023, before retreating to close at about 4.96%. The 2-year yield rose to about 4.65% and the 30-year to around 5.34%.

Oil added to the pressure. Brent settled at $105.68 and WTI at $101.39 after Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia and an attack on a Saudi east-west oil pipeline that bypasses the Strait of Hormuz. That route could affect as much as 4% of global supply. No economic data was released.

## What to watch

The Fed's two-day meeting concludes Wednesday, and markets expect a quarter-point hike. The question is how the projections look. On oil, the Saudi pipeline repair timeline and any sign of Gulf talks resuming will matter more than the headline price.
