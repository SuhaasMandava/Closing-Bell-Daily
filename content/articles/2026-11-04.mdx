The market welcomed gridlock. The Dow soared 3.03%, or 1,569.40 points, to 53,356.12, the S&P 500 gained 1.58% to 7,688.62 and the Nasdaq 1.44% to 25,736.51. Democrats took the House and the Senate, a setback for President Trump that investors read as reducing policy uncertainty. Nine of 11 sectors rose, and advancers beat decliners 3 to 1.

Crude fell more than 8% on hopes that the political shift would speed an end to the Iran war. Energy fell 4.8%.

Amazon rose 6.1% and Microsoft 6.7%. Nvidia fell 3.2% and Micron 7%, as concern about tighter oversight of AI infrastructure weighed on related businesses. The VIX fell 20.3% to 16.61. The 10-year yield dropped 5.8 basis points to 4.89%.

## The number behind it

**3.03%.** The Dow's one-day gain was its largest in this stretch, and 27 of its 30 stocks rose. It shows how much of the market had been waiting on the election, and how much the rally depended on cyclicals rather than AI.

The details: committee leadership, any proposals on data centers and AI, and what Wednesday's rally means for Thursday.
