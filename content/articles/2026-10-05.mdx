A calm start to the week with a record to show for it. The Nasdaq Composite gained 1.05% to 27,477.31, topping the 27,244.28 close set on September 22. The S&P 500 rose 0.66% to 7,773.95 and the Dow added 91 points to 51,267.90. Ten of the 11 sectors rose.

Nvidia gained 2.1% and Microsoft 1.5%, accounting for much of the Nasdaq's lead over the Dow.

## What moved

| Sector | Change | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Materials | +1.2% | Led the S&P 500 |
| Communication Services | +1.1% | Mega-cap strength |
| Energy | +0.9% | Oil firm |
| Real Estate | -0.4% | Only decliner |

Seventeen Dow components rose and 12 fell. The VIX rose 1.4% to 15.52, and volume was light at 16.8 billion shares against a 20-day average of 17.3 billion.

## The number behind it

**54.9.** The ISM services index for September came in line with expectations, down from 55.4 in August, still comfortably above the 50 line that separates expansion from contraction. The reading gave neither side of the rate debate a new argument. The 10-year yield closed at 5.31%, up 3.4 basis points.

## What to watch

Wednesday's release of the minutes from the Fed's September meeting. They will show how many officials favor another hike this year, and how firmly. The September CPI report arrives next week, and bank earnings follow.
